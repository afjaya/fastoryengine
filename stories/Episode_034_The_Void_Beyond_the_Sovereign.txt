The gaze of the titan beyond the Sovereign Gate did not feel like vision; it felt like displacement.

Pressure crushed down upon the floating ring of debris, heavy enough to pop the steel rivets along Silas Vance’s pauldron. In the socket of his left eye, the shattered cosmic core throbbed with a agonizing, frozen fever. Cold light—the color of dying nebulae—spilled down his scarred cheekbone, sizzling where it hit the frosted steel of his chestplate. 

"Silas!" Lyra’s voice was stripped thin by the howling atmospheric shear. She clung to an inverted flying buttress, her dual daggers struck deep into the ancient masonry. Blue ley-sparks whistled off her blades as she forced her gaze toward the tear in reality. "Don't look at it! It's matching your resonance!"

Silas couldn't look away. The thing in the starless dark had no face, only a vast, crawling geometric horror of un-light, ribbed with pale, calcified rings that turned like planetary gears. Where its gaze settled upon Silas, the air dissolved into fine black dust. 

A flash of blinding, arrogant gold pierced the gloom.

First Inquisitor Vaelis rode the falling wreckage down from the upper manifold, his white-and-gold solar plate shining like a newly birthed star despite the dark that swallowed the chamber. His heavy halberd slashed through the air, leaving a trail of superheated plasma that scorched the frost off Silas’s boots.

"Heretics!" Vaelis’s voice rang with the artificial resonance of High Council modulation, echoed through the silver speaker-grille of his visor. "You have unsealed the Primordial Well! You have doomed the Citadel!"

"The Council doomed it when they built a siphon on a tomb!" Silas gritted out through clenched teeth. He brought his silver broadsword up just in time. 

The collision of solar-forged steel and ancient runic silver struck a blinding spark that sent shockwaves rippling through the floating stone fragments. Silas’s boots skidded backward across the crumbling edge of the masonry. His left arm numbed instantly under the sheer brute force of the Inquisitor's strike, but he rolled his shoulders, using the momentum to sweep his blade low, aiming for the unarmored joint behind Vaelis’s knee.

Vaelis sneered, stepping clear as floating debris drifted between them. "The Arch-Mage was a fool to graft that wretched stone into your flesh, Vance. You were always a broken tool. Now you are merely an anchor for the end."

"Save the sermon for the Council, Vaelis," Lyra shouted from above. 

She let go of her handhold, dropping through the low-gravity void with precise, terrifying grace. She landed squarely on Vaelis’s back plate, plunging her left dagger into the gap beneath his helm. Runic light flared—a brilliant, electric violet—as she severed the mana-feed to his armor’s kinetic barriers.

Vaelis roared, a sound of muffled, indignant fury. The golden aura surrounding him flickered wildly. He swung his elbow back, catching Lyra across the ribs with a crack that sounded like snapping pine. She lost her grip, tumbling backward onto the trembling lip of the Sovereign Gate's outer rim.

"Lyra!" Silas lunged forward.

The void entity reacted to the surge of kinetic force. A low, subsonic thrum reverberated through the marrow of Silas’s bones. The atmospheric pressure plummeted to zero in an instant, drawing the air directly out of Silas’s lungs. Frost swept across his visor in intricate, crystalline webs. 

From the center of the rift, a tendril of pure, solid shadow—thick as a cathedral spire and encrusted with glowing, dead runes—thrust into the physical world. It struck the central archway of the Sovereign Gate, splintering stone that had stood since the First Epoch.

The force of the impact tossed Vaelis across the void like a ragged doll, his golden plate dented and leaking trails of radiant white mana. 

Silas dropped to his knees, his broadsword planting point-down into the stone to keep himself from being sucked into the gaping tear. The corrupted ocular core in his left socket burned violently, straining against his optic nerve as if trying to claw its way out of his skull to join the nightmare on the other side. 

*Brother...* 

A voice echoed not in his ears, but directly inside the icy, calcified center of his brain. It was layered, vast, and horribly familiar. It carried the cadence of Arch-Mage Thai, but drowned in the resonant hum of a thousand dead worlds.

*Thai?* Silas thought, his knuckles whitening on the grip of his broadsword as he fought the mental intrusion. *Thai is dead. I watched him burn in the Mana Eclipse.*

*He was the key,* the voice vibrated, causing Silas’s vision to split into sickening double images. *He gave you the eye to keep the gate locked from within. But you brought it back to the keyhole, knight. You brought us home.*

"Silas!" Lyra was on her feet, clutching her side, blood seeping through her fingers where her leather tunic was torn. She crawled toward the primary keystone of the Gate, her eyes wild, sweeping over the rapidly shifting runes engraved into the ancient arch. "It's not a portal! It's a lock-ring that's been turned backward!"

"Can you fix it?" Silas spat, a mixture of blood and frost trailing from his lips.

"The Council inverted the ley lines to draw power up into the upper Citadel!" Lyra yelled over the deafening roar of rushing space-time. "They broke the counter-weight! If I align the secondary runes, I can trigger a localized collapse, but I need a massive spark of pure astral mana to trip the circuit!"

"I don't have astral mana," Silas growled, thrusting himself upward, his plate armor clanking heavily in the erratic gravity. "I have a cursed eye and broken iron."

"Your eye *is* the spark!" She pointed her runic dagger at the archway above them. "Thai didn't graft a void-shard into you, Silas! That’s an Astral Core—a concentrated piece of the Gate's original power supply! It’s corrupted because it’s been separated from the ring for ten years!"

Before Silas could digest the revelation, the air behind them detonated with light.

Inquisitor Vaelis rose from the floating rubble, his helm discarded to reveal a face covered in burning, gold-inlaid sigils. His eyes bled pure radiance. His halberd hummed with an unstable, white-hot crest of raw solar magic.

"I will not let a rat from the slums and a traitorous dog seal the fate of Aethelgard!" Vaelis screamed, his voice warped by the sheer volume of magic pouring through his veins. "The High Council will harvest this realm, as was foreordained!"

Vaelis charged, his boots leaving scorching footfalls on thin air as he leaped across the chasm, swinging his halberd in a wide, decapitating arc meant to cleave both Silas and the keystone in two.

Silas didn't raise his sword to parry.

He reached up with his gauntleted left hand and tore off his eye patch.

The bare, shattered ocular core burst into full, terrifying brilliance. A beam of unstable, dark-astral light erupted from his socket, striking the blade of Vaelis’s halberd point-blank. The collision was instantaneous and catastrophic. The solar magic inverted, turning back on its caster. Vaelis’s armor ruptured from the inside out, blinded by the very radiance he sought to wield.

"Now, Lyra!" Silas bellowed, his voice straining under the unimaginable agony of channeling the core's raw output. 

Lyra didn't hesitate. She threw herself toward the archway, driving both of her runic daggers into the central alignment node. She caught the beam of dark-astral light spilling from Silas’s eye with the flat of her blade, redirecting the torrent of cosmic energy directly into the ancient ley channels of the Sovereign Gate.

The ancient runes along the archway snapped from sickly violet to brilliant, pristine azure.

The titan beyond the rift uttered a sound that shattered the floating masonry into fine gray sand—a sound of profound, ancient frustration. The calcified rings of the entity began to pull back as the edges of the Sovereign Gate shuddered, its structural geometry snapping back into alignment. The tear in reality began to compress, folding in on itself like a dying star.

"Silas! It's collapsing!" Lyra shrieked, grabbing his arm as the floor beneath them split wide open.

Vaelis, screaming as his scorched armor disintegrated, was dragged backward by the immense gravitational pull of the closing rift. He reached out, his burning hand snapping around Silas’s ankle, dragging the knight toward the closing tear.

"If I burn," Vaelis snarled, his face peeling away into light, "you burn with me, Vance!"

Silas brought his silver broadsword down with all the strength left in his body. The runic blade severed Vaelis’s gauntlet at the wrist. The Inquisitor slipped backward, swallowed instantly by the snapping jaws of the closing void.

The Sovereign Gate slammed shut with a concussive shockwave that sent a ripple through the fundamental laws of physics. Light vanished. Gravity vanished.

The suspended platform completely disintegrated.

Silas felt himself falling through absolute darkness, the wind howling past his ears as he plunged deeper into the uncharted lower depths beneath the Citadel. He reached blindly through the blackness until his arm caught Lyra’s tunic, pulling her close as the rushing air grew stiflingly hot.

Below them, deep within the planetary crust where no citizen of the Astral Citadel had ever set foot, a faint, rhythmic pulsing began to glow.

It was not gold like the Council’s solar fire, nor black like the void. It was pale, emerald, and organic.

*Welcome to the engine,* the voice whispered one final time in Silas’s mind, fading slowly into silence.

The darkness gave way to a vast, underground biome of massive, crystalline roots creeping around an ancient, slumbering leviathan made of stone and light—and perched atop its massive skull was the crest of the Fallen Arch-Mage.

Silas and Lyra slammed into the ancient canopy of glowing roots, tumbling into the abyss below.
