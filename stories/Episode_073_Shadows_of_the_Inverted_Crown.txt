It fixed upon the small, broken alcove where the disgraced knight and the stargazer hid.

Silas’s left socket burned with agonizing, liquid heat. He gasped, dropping to one knee as the dark essence inside him surged in terrible resonance with the beast. Black blood, thin and smelling of crushed ozone, trickled down his cheek from beneath the rim of his eye.

"Then don't miss your read," Silas said quietly.

Silas stepped between the Inquisitors and Lyra. The heat from his left eye was blinding now, filling half his field of vision with shifting, dark constellations.

"Lyra," Silas said, his voice terrifyingly calm. "Prepare the arch."
