*THEY BUILT THEIR HEAVEN UPON MY SPINES, LITTLE SPARKS. WILL YOU LET THEM BLEED ME DRY UNTIL THE REALMS CRUMBLE TO ASH?*

"Look at these channels. The Citadel has been draining this creature for three centuries. The whole city up there... it’s not anchored to the sky, Silas. Silas immediately shifted his stance, placing himself between Lyra and the shadow cast by a broken balustrade. "Show yourself," he commanded, his boots finding traction on the uneven basalt.

"Now, Silas!" she yelled.

"Look down."

The floating graveyard had grown quiet, but the air was no longer cold. A suffocating, dry heat was rising from the depths. 

Far beneath their floating rock, miles into the absolute floor of the spatial void, the darkness was parting. A ring of colossal, ancient stone pillars—each the size of a mountain peak, wrapped in shattered golden chains—glowed red-hot. 

And between those pillars, an eye opened.

It was not a eye of flesh, but a burning, catastrophic iris formed of compressed, dying stars and collapsing nebulae. It stretched across the entire width of the abyss, its immense pupil locked directly onto Silas.

The atmospheric pressure dropped to zero. The basalt rib beneath their feet fractured with a deafening crack, splitting down the center. 

*THE LAST CHAIN IS WEAK, SILAS VANCE,* the entity’s voice thundered through his marrow, shaking the teeth in his jaw, burning through his mind with apocalyptic clarity. *BREAK IT, AND BE FREE. LET IT HOLD, AND I WILL DRAG YOUR CITADEL DOWN INTO THE FIRE WITH ME.*
