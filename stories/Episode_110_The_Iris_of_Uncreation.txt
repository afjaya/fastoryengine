The air did not merely break; it curdled. Gravity relinquished its hold as the foundation of the Citadel’s core sheared away, turning thousands of tons of ancient, runic-carved marble into a terrifying avalanche of stone and burning conduits. 

Falling through the freezing abyss, Sir Silas Vance kicked hard off a falling balustrade, lunging through the haze of pulverized mortar. He grabbed Lyra Thorne by the collar of her leather doublet, hauling her inward as he drove the tip of his heavy silver broadsword into the flank of a plunging iron support girder. Sparks exploded in a blinding, violet spray, violent friction roaring through his shoulder joints until the blade caught in a rivet line. They swung in a wide, nauseating arc, suspended over an endless black sky.

Beneath them, miles below where the dark ribs of the lower district dissolved into the unmapped abyssal depths, the eye burned.

It was an orb formed not of organic tissue, but of compressed void-oil and dying constellations, sprawling broad enough to swallow whole cities. The iris was a slowly turning ring of silver runes that predated language itself. As it opened, the atmospheric pressure plummeted. The sudden vacuum pulled the breath from Silas’s lungs, tasting of ozone, ancient iron, and the bitter bite of absolute zero. 

The entity’s gaze locked directly onto Silas’s left socket.

Inside his skull, the stolen cosmic essence—the unstable ember he had carried since the forbidden ritual at Oakhaven—began to boil. His left eye blazed with a sickening, gold-black radiance, casting harsh, long shadows across the underside of the ruined girder. Pain, sharp and freezing, spiked into his frontal lobe, compelling him to look away, yet his neck refused to obey.

"Silas!" Lyra’s voice was clipped, raspy against the sudden drop in atmospheric density. She swung her legs around the girder, finding a desperate foothold on a bent crossbeam. "Do not match its pulse! It's trying to equalize through your eye!"

"It's... drawing..." Silas spat, blood mixing with dark, shimmering fluid that leaked from his left socket. His knuckles turned white on the pommel of his sword. "It thinks I'm a fragment... returning home."

"You aren't a fragment, you stubborn old fool, you're an anchor!" Lyra jammed her left runic dagger directly into a fracture on the girder. The blue etched runes along her blade flared brightly, reading the frantic, tangled flows of celestial mana surging around them. "Look up! Look at the core ring!"

Silas forced his right eye—his human eye—to squint upward through the falling debris. Above them, high in the hollow cavern of the Citadel’s underbelly, the High Council's grand purge mechanisms were firing. Great rings of gilded brass and siphoning glass spun in frantic counter-rotation. The purge was not designed to merely erase the starving lower districts; it was an extraction engine. 

 suspended in the center of those rings was the grotesque, mutated frame of Arch-Mage Thai. Tens of thousands of thick, glowing conduits pierced his petrified flesh, drawing out his diluted blood to act as a cosmic bridge. 

"They aren't trying to destroy the Sovereign," Lyra snarled, her short dark hair whipping around her face in the violent updrafts. "They’re milking it! Thai isn't the seal anymore—he's the tap! The Council is draining the Sovereign's ancient mana straight into the upper spires to power their own immortality, and the strain is tearing the spatial fabric in half!"

A low, subterranean hum vibrated through the steel girder beneath them. It was not a sound heard through the ears, but felt directly within the marrow of their bones. The First-Eon Sovereign exhaled.

The force of the cosmic breath hit them like a physical wall. The girder groaned, its structural welds snapping one by one under the terrifying weight of compressed space. Silas felt his grip slipping. The silver runes on his broadsword flickered and died, suppressed by the sheer density of the Sovereign's primordial aura. 

"We can't stay on this beam!" Silas roared over the sound of grinding metal. "When the next core ring collapses, this whole framework drops into the iris!"

"There!" Lyra pointed toward a narrow, jutting aqueduct thirty feet to their left—a relic of the old drainage system that emptied into the under-slums. "If I can short the ley line running through that conduit, the inverted gravity will catch us for three seconds! But you have to sever Thai’s tether, or the spatial collapse will drag the entire lower city into the void!"

"Sever the tether?" Silas barked a grim, breathy laugh. "Lyra, Thai is three hundred feet above us and wrapped in high-council shielding!"

"Then hit him with your eye!" Lyra kicked off the beam, dropping three feet onto a narrower strut. She drew her second dagger with fluid grace, tracing a blinding streak through the air. "You've been holding that corrupted power back for ten years because you were afraid it would consume you! Consume it first, Silas!"

She didn't wait for his answer. Lyra slammed both runic blades deep into the exposed mana vein running along the aqueduct wall. 

A brilliant shockwave of sapphire light exploded outward. The immediate gravitational pull inverted violently. The falling rubble halted mid-air, suspended in a surreal field of absolute stillness. Dust drifted upward like fine silver sand.

Silas didn't hesitate. He unclasped his heavy plate shoulder-guards, letting the dead weight drop into the dark, and let go of his sword’s hilt, catching the weapon in a two-handed low guard. He drew a deep, burning breath of cosmic mana, surrendering control to the dark ember inside his skull.

The world went monochrome. His human eye saw only darkness, but his left eye—the cosmos-born parasite—saw the universe laid bare. Lines of brilliant, toxic-gold mana crisscrossed the cavern like the threads of a spider's web, all converging on the tortured figure of Arch-Mage Thai high above. 

*Brother...* a voice echoed in the back of Silas's mind, brittle and distant. It was Thai, or what was left of him within the council's grand machine. *Strike the node... free us both...*

"Hold fast, Lyra!" Silas growled.

He drove the pommel of his broadsword into his own chest armor, using the silver metal as a lightning rod. He opened his left eye completely, tearing away the magical seals he had forged over a decade of exile.

A beam of pure, unrefined cosmic radiation shot from his left eye, striking the flat of his broadsword. The silver metal shrieked, glowing blindingly white as it focused the chaotic beam into a razor-thin lance of astral light. With a deafening roar, Silas swept the blade upward in a brutal, rising strike.

The arc of focused cosmic energy sheared through the night air, slicing straight through the gilded siphoning rings above. 

Glass shattered with the sound of a thousand breaking mirrors. The thick, blood-filled conduits attached to Arch-Mage Thai severed instantly, whipping through the air like loose cables on a storm-tossed ship. Thai’s petrified body gave a final, violent spasm before disintegrating into a cloud of glowing, pale-blue ash.

Without its living tap, the siphon engine failed.

The inverted gravity field collapsed in an instant. The sudden return of natural force slammed Silas hard against the aqueduct’s stone ledge. He fell to his knees, coughing up dark crimson, his left eye bleeding profusely, completely blind in that socket now. 

Lyra collapsed beside him, her daggers cracked and smoking, her hands trembling violently from the feedback. "Did... did we cut it?" she gasped, clutching her ribs.

"Thai is dead," Silas rasped, wiping blood from his chin with the back of his gauntlet. "The siphon is broken."

"Silas..." Lyra’s voice lost its sharp edge, replaced by a sudden, chilling dread. She looked over the edge of the aqueduct. "The siphon didn't seal the eye."

Silas forced himself up, leaning heavily on his broadsword. 

Below them, the gargantuan eye of the First-Eon Sovereign was no longer merely watching. Deprived of the siphon that had kept it tethered in a state of suspended sleep, the ancient entity was waking up fully. 

The planetary iris dilated. A mass of vast, ink-black tendrils, each as thick as a fortress wall and lined with glowing celestial runes, writhing out from the depths of the iris. They rose into the underbelly of the Citadel like the fingers of a drowning god, wrapping around the massive stone pillars that supported the upper city.

The foundation of Aethelgard gave a sickening, prolonged groan. Above them, the spires of the High Council began to tilt. Down below, the dark tendrils crested the edges of their aqueduct, surrounding Silas and Lyra in a towering cage of void-flesh and ancient power.

From the depths of the iris, a single, terrible thought projected into Silas's mind, heavy enough to crack his ribs:

*YOU HAVE BROKEN THE CAGEMASTER. NOW, GIVE ME MY EYE.*

High above, the High Council's secondary purge array began to charge, aiming a massive beam of destruction straight down through the core toward the awakening beast—with Silas and Lyra trapped directly in the line of fire.
