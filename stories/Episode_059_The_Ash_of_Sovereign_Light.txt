The air in the Slag-Drains smelled of burnt copper and ancient, curdled mana. Black grease dripped from rusted iron catwalks high above, sizzling as it struck the glowing blue runoff channels that carved through the Astral Citadel's lowest sub-levels. 

Sir Silas Vance spat blood onto the wet iron grate beneath his boots. His chest burned—not with the clean heat of a Solar Knight's inner furnace, but with the suffocating, frigid pulse of a second heart. The Sovereign's shattered soul was nestled inside his fractured core like broken glass in an open wound, grinding against every breath he took. His left eye flared wildly, casting long, violent purple shadows across the narrow drainage corridor.

"Six of them," Lyra whispered beside him, her back pressed hard against a damp, conduit-covered pillar. Her breath bloomed white in the sudden, dramatic drop of ambient temperature. Her eyes, wide and luminous with the faint gold ring of her stargazer sight, tracked the invisible web of mana warping through the corridor. "Inquisitorial Purifiers. Heavy null-plate. They aren't here to capture us, Silas. They’re running a clean-sweep purge pattern."

The lead Purifier stepped into the dim light of a flickering rune-lamp. His iron mask was smooth and featureless, devoid of eye slits—guided only by the humming sensor-arrays embedded in his collarbones. In his armored hands, a seven-foot suppression halberd hummed a dead, flat note that made Silas's teeth ache. The null-field was already expanding, suffocating the ambient ley lines and turning the surrounding air thick as oil.

"They always send the ones who can't hear the screaming," Silas muttered. He shifted his grip on his heavy broadsword. The leather wrap on the hilt was slick with his own sweat. "Keep behind me, girl."

"If I stay behind you, your backswing will take my head off," she snapped softly, drawing her dual runic daggers with a dry *shhhk*. The silver etchings along her blades flared pale green, reacting to the violent stress in the local magical weave. "Their formation is locked to the captain’s left shoulder. That null-relay box. Break the relay, and the suppression field collapses."

"Good to know," Silas said, and took a heavy step forward.

The boots of the Purifiers struck the iron grate in unison—a rhythmic, metallic *clang, clang, clang*. The lead knight didn't issue a surrender demand. There were no words in the Purifier protocol for those marked by a class-zero containment breach. There was only total erasure.

The captain lunged, the heavy tip of his halberd carving a white-hot arc of suppressive force through the gloom. Silas brought his broadsword up to parry. The impact rattled through his collarbones—a dull, deadened thud as the null-rune on the halberd collided with his blade's etched silver. The null-field instantly bit into Silas’s magic, trying to smother the fire in his veins. 

It did not find the orderly, disciplined mana of a Solar Knight. It touched the ancient, corrupted nightmare of the Sovereign.

Silas’s left eye exploded with blinding violet light. A sound like grinding glaciers roared inside his skull, and a chorus of a thousand dead voices echoed through his marrow: *YOU WOULD BEND TO IRON? UNMAKE THEM.*

The air pressure in the corridor violently collapsed. A sudden vacuum hit Silas's ears with a sharp *pop*.

Gritting his teeth until he felt enamel crack, Silas fought the entity trying to pilot his limbs. Instead of letting the cosmic wave rip his body apart, he funneled the violent, freezing energy straight down his arms into the broadsword. The silver metal didn't glow—it turned absolute black, light itself drowning in the density of its newly altered mass.

Silas ripped his blade sideways, shattering the Purifier’s halberd shaft into a dozen splinters of smoking iron.

"Now, Lyra!" Silas roared.

Lyra blurred past Silas’s left side, her small frame diving beneath a second Purifier's sweeping morningstar. Her left dagger struck the joint of the lead captain's pauldron with surgical precision, driving the runic tip straight into the delicate weave of the null-relay.

A harsh, screeching pop echoed through the drains. Bright blue arcs of wild feedback erupted from the relay box, frying the captain's upper armor and throwing him backward into the sewage trench.

"Relay down!" Lyra shouted, rolling back to her feet as another Purifier lunged at her flank. She parried with both daggers crossed, the impact sending her skidding back across the slick metal floor. "Silas! The core in your chest—it's leaking into the drain channels!"

Silas barely heard her. Two Purifiers closed on him, their halberds crossing in an X-lock to pin him against the vault wall.

*Crush them,* the Sovereign’s cold voice commanded from within. *They are mud. You are the star.*

"Get out of my head!" Silas snarled aloud. His voice layered into a horrifying dual timbre—his own gravelly rasp laced with the resonant, metallic echo of the fallen monarch.

He slammed his off-hand against the central crossguard of his broadsword. The unstable cosmic core in his chest gave a sickening lurch, and a wave of compressed, violet gravitational force erupted outward in a thirty-foot ring.

The blast hit the Purifiers like a falling moon.

Their heavy iron armor crumpled like wet parchment. Their weapons shattered into fine dust. The shockwave blew the remaining Purifiers through the heavy iron railings, sending their broken forms crashing forty feet down into the boiling mana-waste below. The blast did not stop there—it struck the ancient, load-bearing pillars of the Slag-Drains, cracking the centuries-old bedrock of the Astral Citadel itself.

Silence settled over the corridor, broken only by the steady *drip-drip-drip* of leaking mana-fluid and Silas's ragged, shallow breathing.

He collapsed to one knee, driving the point of his broadsword deep into the iron grate to keep from falling face-first into the sludge. Black steam rose from his plate armor. The veins across his neck and left cheek were no longer red—they were glowing, translucent threads of dark violet light.

Lyra rushed to his side, her daggers stowed, hands hovering over his shoulders as if afraid that touching him might trigger another explosion. "Silas... look at me."

"I'm... still here," Silas growled, his left eye pulsing with a terrifying, rhythmic light that matched no star in the known firmament. "Barely."

"You crushed their suppression grid with raw gravitational density," she said, her voice trembling slightly as she stared at the cracked stone wall behind him. "That isn't Solar magic. That's... First Era Void-Weaving. Silas, what did that thing do to your soul?"

"Fused," he grunted, forcing himself up. His leg armor groaned under the sheer weight of his strained body. "It didn't just touch my core. It anchored itself. If I try to purge it now, it takes my heart with it."

Before Lyra could answer, the deep bedrock beneath their boots began to vibrate.

It was not the random tremor of collapsing stone. It was a rhythmic, mechanical thrum—a massive, ancient pulse deep within the foundations of the lower Citadel. The blue runoff in the drainage channels suddenly inverted, flowing *backward* up the walls in defiance of gravity.

Along the length of the dark corridor, ancient runes—crude, jagged symbols that pre-dated Aethelgard and the High Council by ten thousand years—ignited in blinding, crimson lines.

"Oh, no," Lyra whispered, her eyes tracking the glowing lines as they raced across the ceiling. "The Sovereign's mana discharge... it didn't just wreck the Purifiers. It woke the lower containment sub-reactor."

"Is that bad?" Silas asked grimly, tightening his bloody grip on his sword.

"The High Council built the Citadel on top of a dormant Engine of the Unmade," Lyra said, her face turning pale in the harsh red glow of the awakening runes. "They use the upper drains to keep it smothered in cold slag. Your core just sent a full Sovereign-level pulse directly into its intake array. It thinks its old master has returned."

A deafening screech of rending metal echoed from the far end of the corridor. The massive, sealed blast doors leading deeper into the Citadel's forbidden sub-levels—doors that had remained locked since the First Era—began to grind upward.

From the abyss beyond the doors came no sound of wind or water. Only a low, rhythmic thrum like a giant brass heart beating... and the sudden, terrifying drop of atmospheric pressure that presaged a localized cosmic implosion.

High above them, emergency sirens began to wail through the drainage shafts. A harsh, synthesized voice resonated from the Citadel's alarm relays:

*ALERT. CLASS-ZERO CONTAINMENT BREACH DETECTED IN SUB-LEVEL SEVEN. ANCIENT CORE PROTOCOL: UNSEALED. INITIATING HIGH-ORBITAL PURGE-BEAM RECHARGING SEQUENCE IN THREE MINUTES.*

Lyra looked up at the ceiling, then down at the yawning black abyss of the opening blast doors. "We have three minutes before the High Council vaporizes this entire sector from orbit to hide the breach."

Silas stared into the glowing red dark of the unsealed engine room. He could feel the pulse in his chest beating in perfect, terrifying synchronization with the ancient machine below.

"Then we aren't going up," Silas said, his voice cold and resolute as he stepped toward the open threshold. "We're going in."
