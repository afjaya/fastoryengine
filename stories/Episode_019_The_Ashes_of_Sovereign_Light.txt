The world broke into a thousand singing knives of glass. 

Air ripped upward with the violence of a dying storm as the floor collapsed, casting Sir Silas Vance, Lyra Thorne, and the gilded vanguard of the High Council into the roaring abyss of the containment well. White-hot solar air scorched Silas’s lungs through his visor. Below them, the imprisoned Solar Sovereign burned—a chained star suspended in an ocean of dark, pressurized void-mana, its dying radiance blinding even through closed eyelids.

"Silas!" Lyra’s scream was ripped away by the howling draft. 

Silas kicked against a falling slab of runic obsidian, tumbling through the rush of glass shards. His left eye—the shattered, cosmic core grafted beneath his socket—flared violently. It pulsed in agonized resonance with the god below, painting his field of vision in veins of liquid violet and crimson. Ley lines dragged at his flesh like invisible hooks. 

He lunged out, his heavy gauntlet catching the strap of Lyra’s leather harness. He dragged her close, twisting his body to take the impact as they plummeted toward a massive, floating ring of calcified stone that encircled the central monolith.

Below them, Inquisitor-General Vane plummeted like a fallen meteor, his mirrored plate catching the golden death-flare of the Sovereign. Vane slammed his sun-spear into the vertical face of a support pillar, sparks shooting in a ten-foot ribbon as he ground to a halt fifty feet above the lower platform. Three of his vanguards were not as fortunate; they struck the stone ledge with sickening, armor-crunching thuds, their bodies instantly enveloped by the lingering thermal wash of the core.

Silas hit the stone hard.

The impact drove the wind from his chest in a red mist of agony. His pauldrons shrieked against the calcified floor, sliding fifteen yards through ash and glittering glass before coming to a stop at the very edge of the inner drop. 

Lyra rolled off him, gasping, her dual daggers already unsheathed and humming with pale blue light. Her face was smeared with soot and blood where a glass shard had grazed her cheekbone, but her gray eyes were wide, tracking the torrents of raw mana flooding the air.

"The core is destabilizing!" she spat, her voice raspy from the heat. She pushed herself up, squinting through the ambient distortion. "Silas, look at the alignment! The monolith isn't just siphon energy—it’s siphoning the Sovereign's *concept*. It’s drinking its divinity to prime the purge matrix above!"

Silas pushed himself to his knees, his silver broadsword grinding against the floor as he used it for leverage. His plate armor felt like a furnace baked directly against his skin. Every breath tasted of scorched copper and high-voltage ozone. 

In his left eye, the world was a nightmare of weeping gold and void-black tendrils. The Solar Sovereign hung three hundred feet below their ledge, bound in colossal chains forged from dense starlight. The god was no longer a golden deity; it was a blistered, dying remnant, its skin cracked like dry riverbeds, leaking rivers of liquid solar fire into the dark monolith rooted at the well’s floor.

"They're burning a god to bleach a city," Silas gritted out through clenched teeth. His hand trembled on the hilt of his sword as his cosmic eye throbbed, sending sharp, needle-like migraines directly into his brainstem. "Thai died trying to stop this..."

A heavy boot crushed a fragment of glass fifty feet away.

Out of the rising smoke stepped Inquisitor-General Vane. His silver plate was scorched black across the chest, the noble sigil of Aethelgard melted into an unrecognizable slag of tin and gold. His sun-spear hummed with a vicious, concentrated beam of plasma at its tip. Behind him, two surviving Inquisitors pulled themselves from the rubble, drew their broad-bladed cleavers, and formed a flank.

"You broke the glass, disgraced knight," Vane said. His voice was unnervingly flat, carried by an acoustic rune built into his gorget. "You have accelerated the timetable. The High Council will not mourn the lower sectors. Their dust will choke the sun by nightfall."

"You're feeding your citizens to a corpse, Vane," Silas said, standing to his full height. He planted his boots, shifting his center of gravity. His broadsword’s ancient runes lit up, responding to his ragged mana, casting a cool silver glow across the hot stone. "Look at it. That monolith isn't an engine. It’s an altar."

"It is order!" Vane barked. 

Vane lunged.

The Inquisitor-General moved with the unnatural, terrifying speed of a man pumped full of refined core-essence. The tip of his sun-spear split the air, leaving a line of ionized heat in its wake. Silas swept his broadsword up to parry, the clashing steel producing a deafening crack that echoed down the containment well. The sheer force of Vane’s thrust drove Silas back two steps, his boots leaving dark streaks in the ash.

Lyra didn't wait. She ducked beneath a swinging cleaver from the second Inquisitor, her left dagger biting deep into the gap at his knee armor. Sparks and spray-blood erupted. She twisted, using the man's falling weight as a springboard to vault toward the dark monolith’s central conduit.

"The runes!" she called back, her sharp eyes scanning the black stone of the monolith as she landed on an elevated cable bridge. "Silas! The lock isn't at the top! It’s anchored to the Sovereign's heart valve! If we sever the main tether, the purge loses its power source!"

"Touch that tether and your soul burns to ash, scholar!" the third Inquisitor roared, charging her with his cleaver raised high.

Lyra blurred to the left, blade catching the heel of his hand, severing fingers before he could complete the arc. She parried his follow-up backhand with her second dagger, her eyes darting across the ancient celestial script carved into the black stone. 

"It's an ancient sealing dialect," Lyra muttered under her breath, ignoring the bleeding Inquisitor staggering back toward her. She pressed her bare palm against the frozen black iron of the monolith, feeling the terrifying, localized freeze of void-magic against the oppressive heat of the room. "The Council didn't build this... they unearthed it. It’s an anchor from the First Cataclysm!"

Above them, the Citadel screamed.

A sound like grinding tectonic plates echoed down through the shattered ceiling. The Purge Protocol was advancing. Mass automated iron gates began sliding down from the subterranean ceiling, sealing off the lower well sector by sector. Heavy, pressurized locks slammed shut with the dull *thud* of falling iron coffins. Red warning runes ignited along every surface, washing the cavern in a sickening blood-light.

"Total containment in three minutes," a cold, mechanical voice resonated from the Citadel's upper vox-horns. "Purge liquid deployment imminent. All lower sectors marked for total thermal erasure."

Vane gritted his teeth, pressing his spear harder against Silas’s blade. "You see? There is no escape, Vance. We die here, but Aethelgard endures above!"

"Aethelgard is a parasite built on bone," Silas snarled. 

Silas stopped trying to overpower Vane’s blade. Instead, he released his right-hand grip on the hilt, allowing the force of Vane’s spear to slide off his crossguard. The white-hot spear blade whistled past Silas's ear, searing the skin along his neck. 

Silas reached out with his free, armored hand, grabbing Vane by the gorget, dragging the Inquisitor-General face-to-face.

Silas opened his left eye completely.

The eye cracked open like a wound, shedding its eye-patch tether. Unfiltered cosmic essence poured out—a beam of jagged, terrifying violet star-fire that blasted directly into Vane’s silver visor. 

Vane shrieked. The reinforced visor shattered inward as the unstable cosmic magic scorched into his eyes, unraveling his neural mana-pathways in real time. The Inquisitor-General stumbled backward, dropping his sun-spear, clutching at his smoking, melting helm as he fell to his knees, thrashing in blinded agony.

Silas gasped, dropping to one knee as his eye throbbed with unbearable pressure. Veins of silver and purple swelled along his neck and cheek. The agony was blinding, threatening to snap his mind into pieces. 

"Silas!" Lyra dropped from the cable bridge, landing beside him. She grabbed his arm, pulling him toward the edge of the lower ledger where the main tether dragged through the air toward the Solar Sovereign. "The monolith’s containment field is cracking because of the Purge Protocol's power surge! Look!"

Silas forced his right eye open through the searing haze. 

The dark monolith was shuddering. The liquid starlight being drawn from the Sovereign was turning from gold to an unstable, volatile crimson. Huge cascades of purged plasma began pouring out from ceiling vents high above, falling like rivers of liquid sun into the containment well, filling the lower basin with blinding, absolute destruction. The heat was becoming unlivable; Silas's cape caught fire, and he ripped it off, letting it float away into the golden hell below.

"The tether," Silas growled, his voice cracking. "Can you sever it?"

"Not from up here!" Lyra yelled over the deafening roar of the descending plasma waterfall. "The junction is anchored directly inside the Sovereign's chest casing! We have to go down *into* the core ring!"

Behind them, the massive iron purge gates slammed down into their final tracks, completely sealing the ceiling above. The exit was gone. The atmosphere was turning into a sea of fire.

From the glowing depths below, the Solar Sovereign opened a single, colossal eye—a orb of dying, liquid gold the size of a warship. It fixed its gaze directly onto Silas's glowing left eye. A psychic wave of pure, unadulterated anguish washed through Silas’s mind, knocking him to his knees as the god whispered a single, harrowing word into his soul:

*RELEASE.*

Silas looked down into the sea of fire, then at Lyra, whose daggers were glowing blindingly hot in the escalating pressure.

"Hold on," Silas rasped, grabbing her leather belt.

He turned toward the abyss, and leaped into the dying light of the god.
