Before Silas could step forward, a heavy, rhythmic thud echoed from the deep shadows at the far end of the vault. A pair of crimson eyes, cold and merciless, ignited in the darkness.

"You are late, Sir Vance," a smooth, dark voice purred from the shadows. "The extraction is already complete."
