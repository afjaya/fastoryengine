Gravity inverted three feet above the threshold, tearing the breath from Sir Silas Vance’s throat in a ragged, freezing gasp. The descent into the titan’s open chest was no clean fall, but a violent, churning swirl of displaced mass. Air pressure plummeted so fast the leather lining inside Silas’s pauldrons groaned, and the silver runes carved down the spine of his heavy broadsword flared a desperate, icy white against the encroaching dark. Below them, the titan’s heart did not merely beat; it thudded with the bone-shattering density of a collapsing moon, sending shockwaves of ozone and cold, crystallized mana surging through the vault.

Silas twisted in mid-air, driving the tip of his broadsword straight into the petrified marrow of a ribs-wide stone archway. Sparks of blue stellar plasma sprayed across his iron visor as the runic blade carved a jagged furrow through ancient bone, slowing his drop just enough. Beside him, Lyra Thorne hooked her dual runic daggers into a web of hardened, calcified arteries. Her boots scraped violently against the slick, violet-tinged stone before she swung her weight onto a narrow, bone-dust ledge.

Silas hit the deck hard. Heavy plate armor met calcified god-flesh with a sickening crunch. He rolled onto his side, his left arm rising instantly to brace his head as his ruined socket erupted in agonizing, blinding heat.

"Silas!" Lyra scrambled over sharp crystalline ridges, her breath coming in ragged hitches. The faint hum of celestial magic was no longer a hum here—it was a physical weight, pressing down on their skulls like an ocean of liquid lead. "Get up! The grav-well is stabilizing, but the air—"

"I have the air," Silas grunted through clamped teeth, pushing himself up onto one knee. He dragged his heavy steel gauntlet away from his face, gasping as cold mana burned his lungs.

Where his left eye had been, a violent flare of cosmic indigo burned through his flesh. The iris was gone, replaced by a swirling vortex of ancient stellar fire that mirrored the massive, pulsing sphere suspended in the center of the vault.

"Look at it," Lyra whispered, her voice sharp with horror despite her awe.

The titan’s heart hung thirty yards wide in the center of a cavernous ribcage lined with brass and shadow. It was a dying star trapped in living muscle, veined with calcified gold and pulsing with a light so brilliant it pained the retina. But the divine flesh was not free. Driven deep into the raw muscle were dozens of massive, wrought-iron siphons, anchored by pulsing brass rings that hummed with Aethelgard’s glyphs of suppression. Thick conduit pipes, wide as siege engines, coiled out from the heart like metal parasites, swallowing the raw, silvery mana and pumping it upward into the Citadel’s spine.

"They aren't just harvesting it," Silas said, his voice raspy as he spat a mouthful of copper-tasting blood onto the glass-like floor. He forced himself to his feet, gripping the heavy hilt of his broadsword with both hands to stop the shaking. "They’re bleeding it dry. Every district, every floating spire... built on a corpse that hasn't finished cooling."

"It *is* cooling," Lyra corrected fiercely, her pale eyes wide as she traced the air with her fingers. Silver lines of light, invisible to ordinary men, danced across her vision as she read the ley flows tearing through the air. "The structural pressure is off the charts. The High Council has pushed the harvest past the threshold. The magical density in these conduits is destabilizing—that's what triggered the Harvester!"

A horrific screech of tearing iron echoed from the vault ceiling above.

Through the jagged rift created by the floor's collapse, the automated Harvester descended. It was a towering nightmare of tarnished bronze and blackened steel, forged by the Council’s ancient artificers. Its central carapace glowed with aggressive red sigils, and six jointed, razor-tipped legs scraped down the support cables, shearing stone and iron into a rain of white-hot sparks. At the end of its central arm hung a spinning array of arc-welding blades and siphon spikes, whirling with a deafening, mechanical roar.

"It thinks we're structural contaminants," Lyra snapped, drawing her daggers with a metallic shriek. The blue runes along their edges flickered erratically, buffeted by the crushing mana field coming off the core. "Or it's here to seal the leak by killing everything in the room."

"Get behind the control spire!" Silas barked, stepping directly between Lyra and the descending automaton.

The Harvester did not pause. It dropped the final twenty feet, its heavy iron talons crushing a calcified arterial conduit. A jet of pure, unrefined stellar fluid sprayed into the air, igniting upon contact with the open iron. The resulting heat wave scorched the vault, singeing Silas's grey hair and blistering the leather beneath his armor.

The construct lunged. Its central arc-blade swept downward in a blinding silver arc meant to bisect him.

Silas did not try to parry the full weight of the war-engine. He stepped inside the blade's sweep, his heavy boots biting into the crystalline floor, and brought his broadsword up in a brutal, rising slash. Runic silver struck brass joint. The impact shrieked through his arms, vibrating through his shoulder plates, but his blade sheared clean through one of the machine's secondary hydraulic lines. Black fluid and ozone-scented steam erupted, blinding the construct's forward optical sensors.

"Lyra! The glyphs on the main siphon!" Silas roared, holding off a crushing strike from a bronze leg with the flat of his blade, his muscles straining to the point of tearing. His left eye flared, the indigo flame leaping three inches out of the socket, searing his temple. "Can you override the suppression lock?"

"The runes are an ancient High-Council cipher!" she shouted back, ducking beneath a sweeping leg and plunging her daggers into the machine's knee joint. Arc-light burst around her hands as she twisted the blades. "If I force a bypass without stabilizing the anchor weight, the whole mountain plummets! The Citadel falls into the lower realms!"

"Do it anyway!" Silas growled, driving his steel boot into the Harvester's damaged joint, forcing the massive construct to tilt sideways onto its ruined chassis.

"I can't!" Lyra yelled, her voice trembling with a rare, cold panic as she looked up at the heart. "You don't understand, Silas! Look at the core! Look at what your eye is reacting to!"

Silas turned his head, his vision splitting dangerously. His right eye saw the dying titan’s heart, trapped in iron and brass. But his left eye—the bleeding, cosmic void in his skull—saw through the physical form.

The heart was not merely dying. It was mutating.

Beneath the Council's suppression runes, the titan's flesh was dark, corrupted, and festering with a violent, erratic pattern. The mana being pumped up to Aethelgard was not clean cosmic light—it was a poison, refined and altered to strip the sanity from those who channeled it. The High Council had not just built a city on a god's corpse; they were feeding the city a rot that was slowly turning every Mage and Knight into a tether for something waiting on the other side of the celestial veil.

"It's a trap," Silas breathed, his voice hollow as the atmospheric pressure spiked again. "The whole city... it's an incubation chamber."

Before Lyra could answer, a loud, metallic *CLANG* echoed from the high perimeter catwalks above the chamber.

Flashing golden light cut through the dark smoke. High Inquisitor execution squads, draped in heavy white cloaks and armed with long arc-pikes, poured onto the upper ledges overlooking the heart.

"In the name of the High Council!" a voice bellowed through an amplifying horn, echoing flatly against the bone walls. "Purge the lower vault! Siphon maximum yield!"

"They're forcing a total draw!" Lyra screamed, scrambling back as the control spire began to turn white-hot, its copper veins melting into slag.

The siphons drove four feet deeper into the titan's heart with a sickening, wet crunch. The heart gave a tremendous, horrifying convulsion. A wave of raw, purple spatial distortion blasted outward, shearing the remaining legs off the Harvester like dry kindling and ripping the breath straight from Silas's chest.

Gravity vanished entirely.

The silver broadsword flew from Silas's grip, caught in the vacuum of space tearing open directly inside the heart. His left eye roared with unbearable, blinding agony as an invisible cord of pure cosmic force slammed into his chest, pulling him helplessly off the ledge toward the exposed center of the core.

"Silas!" Lyra shrieked, her daggers carving desperate, futile sparks into the stone as she tried to anchor herself against the rising hurricane of stellar fire.

Silas reached out, his gauntleted hand grasping blindly into the roaring light as his body was dragged across the event horizon of the living star. His eye locked onto the core's center, and for a fraction of a second, through the blinding pain, he heard a sound that was not human speaking directly into his mind.

*Welcome home, Key.*

The spatial rift snapped shut with the thunderous clap of a collapsing realm, plunging the vault into absolute, suffocating darkness.
