Massive, soot-blackened blocks of white marble and shattered stained-glass tracery plunged through the gloom, tumbling past inverted spires that thrust upward like the bleached ribs of a buried sea-beast. High above, where the upper Astral Citadel had stood for three millennia, the burning wreckage of the grand arcana vaults cast long, sickly orange shadows through the subterranean dusk. The air smelled of scorched bronze, dried sulfur, and ozone—a metallic mix thick enough to leave a bitter copper sheen on the back of Silas Vance’s tongue.

Before him, the void construct moved without air or friction. 

It possessed no face, only a narrow, vacant slit set into a helm forged from dull, star-struck iron. Where its heavy neck met its breastplate, a ring of pale blue runes flickered—not with light, but with a sudden, suffocating void that drank the surrounding glare. A jagged blade of null-metal hissed through the dusty air, sweeping straight for Silas’s throat.

Silas brought his runic broadsword up in a desperate, two-handed parry. The force of the blow did not ring with the song of steel; it struck with the dull, bone-rattling thud of a falling anvil. The impact reverberated up through his iron vambraces, sending a shockwave of cold numbness straight into his shoulder joints. Silas’s heavy leather boots skidded back across the ancient obsidian flags, spraying pale sparks that died the millisecond they touched the null-field radiating from the construct.

"Silas! Don't let it close the distance!" Lyra’s sharp voice cut through the ambient roar of falling masonry. 

She was already moving, her boots light and deliberate as she navigated the tilting floor. Her dual runic daggers were unsheathed, their faint violet edges humming with a sharp, high-pitched resonance that visibly thinned the heavy air around her. She ducked beneath a shower of scalding mortar, her amber eyes wide as she read the shimmering, unseen currents flowing through the room. 

"It's not trying to butcher you!" she called out, vaulting a cracked pedestal. "It's an alignment wrench! The engine beneath this chamber thinks your eye is an uncalibrated core-lever!"

Silas’s left eye throbbed in brutal agreement. Beneath the frayed leather patch he had long abandoned, the shattered astral core burned with a horrific, cold light. The agony was not a mere physical ache; it was a violent intrusion that threatened to split his skull from crown to jaw. His vision fractured into two overlapping realities: through his right eye, he saw the crumbling, dust-choked ruins of the mirror city; through his left, he saw the underlying skeleton of the universe—violent, bleeding streams of raw celestial mana twisting like golden serpents straight into the monolithic iron spire at the chamber’s center.

"It's... pulling," Silas grunted through gritted teeth. His breath puffed in gray plumes of frost. The barometric pressure in the chamber was dropping rapidly, making the air in his lungs feel thin and useless. 

Around the construct, frost formed in intricate, geometric fractals across the flagstones. The entity lunged again, its segmented iron gauntlet extending into a five-pronged talon. The runes carved along its forearm flared with a muted, swallowed light, creating an invisible gravitational eddy that dragged the loose stones toward its palm. 

"Look at the base of the monolith!" Lyra yelled, skidding to a halt near a half-shattered balustrade. She thrust one of her daggers toward the central pedestal. "The runes on the upper capstone aren't containment wards—they're intake channels! The Citadel above was never built to crush this place. It was built as a giant straw to bleed this engine dry! Now that the capstone is broken, the engine is priming itself to feed!"

The construct’s blind helm snapped toward her voice with a sickening, mechanical click. Its long null-blade swept around in a blinding horizontal arc, aiming to cleave her at the waist. 

Lyra dropped flat onto her back, sliding across the frost-slicked flags. The null-blade passed inches above her chin, the absolute cold of its edge flash-freezing the loose strands of copper hair framing her face. Before the entity could reset its posture, she planted her heel against its knee armor and drove her right dagger into the joint gap. A bright spray of violet sparks erupted, accompanied by the screech of grinding metal gears.

"Silas! Now!" she gasped, scrambling backward as the construct stumbled.

Silas did not hesitate. He drew deep on the dying embers of his former knightly training, channeling his breath into his core. He drove the pommel of his broadsword toward his center, then forced the wild, unrefined cosmic power leaking from his left eye down through his arms and into the runic steel of his blade. The carved silver glyphs along the blade ignited—not with the clean, golden light of the Solar Order, but with a harsh, corrupted violet glare.

He stepped into the construct’s guard and swung upward with all the weight of his armored shoulders. The edge caught the construct beneath its armpit, right where the heavy iron plates hinged over a dense bundle of squirming, rune-etched copper cables.

A deafening, bell-like tone shrieked through the cavern. 

The force of the impact did not shatter the void-forged iron, but the raw, volatile mana discharging from Silas’s sword surged through the entity’s internal conduit. The construct shuddered violently, its null-field collapsing into a chaotic spray of purple lightning that sizzled as it bit into the stone floor. 

Yet, the victory lasted only a breath.

The sudden discharge acted as a lightning rod for the iron monolith behind them. Deep within the chasm below Aethel-Umbram, a sound began—a low, sub-audible vibration that resonated in the marrow of Silas’s bones rather than his ears. The massive monolith flared with blinding, crimson glyphs, responding to the unsealed core in Silas's head. 

The floor beneath them gave a sickening lurch. The inverted spires suspended from the ceiling above began to twist, the ancient stone groaning like wet timber as the room's gravity buckled. 

"The engine is wringing out the room!" Lyra shouted, her voice laced with rare panic as the floor tilted beneath them at a terrifying angle. Loose stones, broken statues, and burning roof beams began to slide *upward* toward the cavern ceiling, falling into the sky as the inverted gravitational field took hold. "Silas, sever your mana connection! If that engine fully ignites while your core is linked to it, it will pull your entire nervous system into the furnace!"

"I can't!" Silas roared, clutching his left temple with a gauntleted hand. 

The heat behind his socket was unbearable now, like liquid starlight boiling against his optic nerve. Luminescent blue blood began to seep past his knuckles, dripping onto the tilted flags where it hissed and burned tiny, smoking holes into the ancient obsidian. 

The void construct, despite its spattering copper joints, dragged itself forward once more. Driven by the monolith's violent ignition sequence, its primary command took total precedence. It ignored Lyra entirely, its iron talon reaching out to lock around the crossguard of Silas’s broadsword. 

"It's... locking... the circuit!" Silas spat, his muscles straining as he tried to pull the sword back. The cosmic arc jumping between his eye and the blade had turned his gauntlets into magnetic clamps. He couldn't open his fingers. He was fused to the weapon.

"Get off him!" Lyra leaped onto the construct's back, driving both her daggers into the exposed copper wiring at its neck. Superheated steam hissed from the wound, smelling of crushed resin and ozone. 

The entity did not flinch. It simply leaned its immense iron weight backward, stepping off the edge of the fracturing plaza and dragging Silas with it toward the gaping shaft surrounding the monolith.

"Silas!" Lyra screamed.

They tumbled over the edge into the dark heart of the world. Below them, thousands of feet down, a vast, swirling sea of raw cosmic mana churned—a pale, violent sun trapped inside a cage of dead iron. 

Lyra released her grip on the construct’s neck and kicked off its shoulder, her hand catching the leather cuff of Silas’s right glove in mid-air. With her other hand, she slammed her daggers into the sheer obsidian wall of the abyss. The runic steel shrieked against the stone, carving two deep, glowing furrows as it desperately fought their falling momentum.

They slammed against the cliff face, dangling over the blinding maw of the engine core.

Silas hung by Lyra’s single, trembling grip. Below his boots, the void construct plummeted into the swirling violet sea, still clutching his broadsword. But the connection remained unbroken. A thick, brilliant tether of raw blue light stretched upward from the abyss, anchored directly into Silas's left eye socket, pulling on his skull with the terrifying weight of a falling star.

"I can't... hold you..." Lyra choked out, her face pale, blood dripping from her palms down the hilts of her daggers. The stone around her blades was already spiderwebbing with hairline fractures. "The tether is too heavy..."

Above them, the ultimate catastrophe arrived.

The central keystone of the falling Citadel—a colossal, thousand-ton block of carved marble shaped like a weeping angel—finally cleared the cloud of ash above. It descended like an iron mountain, hurtling straight down the axis of the inverted city, blocking out the light, aimed directly for the shaft they hung within.

Silas looked up into Lyra’s strained, amber eyes, then down at the burning star that was slowly stripping away his mind. 

"Cut the glove, Lyra," Silas growled, his voice steady despite the agony tearing his head apart. "Do it now."

Before she could scream her answer, the keystone struck the upper rim of the shaft, and a wave of thunderous darkness swallowed the world whole.
