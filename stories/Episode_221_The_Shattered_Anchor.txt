"The Council didn't give anyone a funeral, Silas," Lyra said softly, walking up beside him. She placed a hand on his shoulder guard, her fingers brushing the fresh frost coating his plate. "They built a meat-grinder under the world and called it salvation."

They advanced together to the lip of the threshold, looking down—or up—into the heart of the shadow Citadel's lowest sub-level.

The cracked core at the center of the sphere twitched. 

Slowly, like a colossal eye unlidization in the deep ocean, the darkness within the Primordial Conduit shifted, turning its suffocating, ancient gaze directly toward the rogue knight standing at the edge of the abyss.

"Silas..." Lyra breathed, stepping back as her runic daggers flickered and went entirely dark. "It... it knows you're here."
