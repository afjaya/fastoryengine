"You're welcome," Lyra snapped softly, her breath coming in fast, sharp puffs. She wiped a smear of dark soot from her cheek and peered over the edge into the chasm. Her eyes narrowed, tracking thin, luminous ley-lines that threaded through the dark like fractured glass. "We didn't escape the siphon. We just landed in its stomach."

Silas pulled his broadsword free from the stone with a dull ring. He stood up slowly, the joints of his greaves groaning under his weight. He looked out into the vast subterranean chamber—the ancient underbelly of the Astral Citadel that the High Council of Aethelgard had spent three centuries erasing from the celestial maps.

Below them, stretched across miles of empty darkness, hung the Solar God. Its massive, majestic form was warped—its golden skin covered in black, pustular veins of Void corruption, its six burning wings pinned to titan-sized anchoring pikes of black iron.

Suddenly, the air pressure dropped drastically. The faint hum of celestial magic turned into a deafening, sub-audible vibration that vibrated through the marrow of Silas’s bones. 

Down in the dark, the chained god lifted its immense head.

*...BROTHER OF THE FRACTURED SPHERE...*

"Silas, don't listen to it!" Lyra grabbed his arm, her runic daggers humming as she felt the dramatic shift in local ley-energy. "The magic here is unstable! The moment you engage with its mind, your shattered core will—"

"They aren't our biggest problem," Silas said softly.

He looked down.

Cascades of stone and dark, burning solar bile rained down into the dark. "Or it destroys the dropships, tears down the Council's siphon, and gives us a way out," Lyra shot back, her jaw set. "Because right now, the silver-clad murderers above us aren't offering a Third Option."

He locked eyes with Lyra, seeing no fear in her sharp gaze—only the cold, lethal calculation of a street-born scholar who refused to die in the dark.

"Cover me," Silas growled.

He turned toward the edge of the platform, lifted his sword high above his head, and let the unstable core behind his eye completely open its gates.
