Glowing red runes flickered to life along the lengths of the brass siphons, turning the crimson mana flowing within them to a volatile, boiling black.

"Not just security arrays," Silas growled, forcing his legs beneath him. His silver sword felt impossibly heavy, the mana within his body vibrating in violent disharmony with his corrupted eye. "Look down."

"We can't fight them all here, Lyra," Silas said, his voice dropping to a somber, steady gravel. "The bridge won't hold if I release a Solar strike. The structural integrity is already shot."

Silas set his jaw, gripped his sword tight, and stared directly into the waking darkness.
