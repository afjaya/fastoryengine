This isn't Aethelgard craft."

Ancient celestial runes—angular, sharp, and pre-dating the First Dynasty—were carved deep into the metal, glowing with a stagnant, amber decay.

Far above them, echoing through miles of shattered stone, came the muffled, terrifying roar of the swarm. Thousands of newly hatched void entities screeched in unison, a chorus of starving madness. But the sound was distant, separated from them by millions of tons of fallen rock and collapsed anchor pillars.

"The seal worked," Silas spat, wiping a smear of hot, silver-tinged blood from his cheekbone. "The anchor pillar collapsed. The upper sectors are isolated."

"Isolated?" Lyra turned her head, her gaze sharp enough to cut plate armor. "We didn't isolate them, Vance. We dropped ourselves into the butcher's basement. Look at the ley lines."

She pointed her dagger toward the center of the vast chamber. Silas squinted through the violet gloom. 

In normal space, celestial ley lines rose toward the sky, feeding the spires of the Astral Citadel and maintaining the realm's atmospheric balance. Here, the lines were inverted. Thin, glowing threads of inverted mana poured *downward* from the ceiling like weeping willow branches, draining into a massive, circular basin set into the center of the floor.

"The High Council didn't just construct a mana-siphon," Lyra murmured, stepping toward the perimeter of the basin with cautious, silent strides. "They were redirecting the natural flow of the outer sectors. Stealing life from the starving slums to feed whatever is down here."

Silas followed her, his boots leaving heavy impressions in the thick dust. His sword remained unsheathed, held low at his side. As they neared the edge of the pit, the atmospheric pressure shifted abruptly. Silas felt his ears pop, a sudden wave of nausea rolling through his gut as his corrupted core violently churned in response to the pressure change.

"Careful," he rasped, resting a heavy gauntlet on Lyra’s shoulder to stay her advance. "My core is reacting. Something in that pit has the same signature as the ritual that ruined me."

Lyra paused at the brink. She looked down, and the faint light of her daggers illuminated an abyssal void.

And from those depths came a sound.

It was not the sharp, shrieking hunger of the void entities above. It was a slow, deep, rhythmic inhalation—a breath so vast that it pulled the air right out of Silas and Lyra’s lungs, dragging dust and ambient mana down into the dark.

The air rushed past them like a cold mountain gale.

*Haaaaaaah.*

The exhalation followed, thick with the scent of ozone, decayed stars, and ancient, stagnant blood. 

"That's not a machine," Lyra whispered, her voice trembling for the first time since the anchor fell. The High Council didn't build the Citadel to govern the realm, Silas. They built it as a cage."

Silas stepped up beside her, his left hand dropping from his face. The violet light of his corrupted eye flared brightly, piercing the deep gloom of the pit.

It was a creature of horrifying, geometric majesty. Its body was composed of fractured, obsidian plate armor that shifted and slid over a core of liquid star-fire. It had no face—only a smooth, blank mask of white gold, split down the center by a jagged fissure that leaked brilliant, blinding radiance. Countless thick, black conduits were pierced directly into its chest, siphoning its bleeding light and pumping it upward into the Citadel’s foundation.

"A First-Born," Silas breathed, his voice dropping into a hollow whisper. "A Sovereign of the Void. They kept it chained here..."

"They were farming it," Lyra corrected, her eyes darting across the ancient script etched into the basin's rim. "The Council wasn't just siphoning energy from the outer sectors to maintain their elevated paradise. They were using the slum's starved mana to keep this thing subdued, while draining its pure cosmic essence to fuel their spellcraft. The void entities above? They aren't an invading army, Silas. They are parasites that hatched from *its* blood."

A heavy, reverberating *CLANG* echoed through the chamber.

One of the white-brass chains, weakened by the gravitational pulse Silas had triggered when he overloaded the anchor pillar above, snapped with the force of a siege engine. The broken link whipped through the air, shattering an ancient monolith before plummeting into the dark.

The titan shifted.

The remaining eleven chains groaned under a sudden, massive surge of tension. The jagged fissure across the creature's golden mask widened by a fraction of an inch. "Silas!" Lyra yelled over the deafening hum of rising power. "Your eye! It’s drawing the discharge!"

The silver broadsword in his hand began to glow red-hot, its runic etchings smoking as they overloaded with raw cosmic force.

Down in the dark, the white-gold mask slowly tilted upward. 

It had no eyes, yet Silas felt a gaze of impossible weight lock directly onto his soul. The entity recognized the energy leaking from his eye—the mark of the very same ancient ritual that had bound it centuries ago.

*“...BROKEN... SEED...”*

*CLANG.*

A second brass chain snapped. The titan raised a massive, armored arm, its obsidian fingers locking onto the edge of the pit. The pressure in the chamber doubled in an instant, pinning Lyra to the ground and threatening to crush Silas's steel armor like parchment.

Above them, the ceiling began to cave in. Below them, the primordial force of the realm was breaking its chains.

Silas forced his head up, his left eye blazing like a dying star, staring down into the golden fissure of the titan's face as the floor beneath them began to drop into the dark.
