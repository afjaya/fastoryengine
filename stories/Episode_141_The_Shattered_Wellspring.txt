Silas looked up. High above, through the swirling dust and drifting wreckage, the remaining Inquisitors were falling back, scrambling toward the heavy iron blast doors of the upper lift. They were leaving them to burn.

"Hold on to something," Silas said softly.

He didn't wait for her answer. The dying Titan was not falling. Liberated from its chains, its massive, starlight-draped form was dissolving into a shimmering sea of nebulae. But deep beneath where the beast had sat—in the absolute darkness exposed by the shattered floor—something else shifted. 

It was not a titan. It was a pair of glowing, crimson eyes, vast enough to swallow whole cities, slowly opening in the deep trenches beneath the world. The High Council had not been using the Citadel to drain a god; they had been using the god to keep something infinitely worse asleep.

And now, the anchor was gone.
